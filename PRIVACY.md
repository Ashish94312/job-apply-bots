# Sendoff privacy policy

*Effective 14 October 2026. Applies to the Sendoff Chrome extension.*

Sendoff applies to jobs for you on Hirist, Instahyre, Wellfound, Cutshort, LinkedIn and Naukri, and on companies' own
application forms on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday, and on LinkedIn can ask people for referrals. Everything it knows stays in your own Chrome. It has no server, no account and no
analytics, and the developer never receives any of your data.

## What Sendoff keeps, and where

All of it is kept in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage`) on your computer:

- **Your settings:** the jobs to search for, your filters, and the answers you write for screening questions.
  These can include personal details you choose to enter, such as your phone number, city, college, notice
  period and salary.
- **Your profile and resume:** the name, email, phone, city, country, links and current job you enter, and the resume
  file you add, for the application forms that ask for them.
- **Answers you gave when it asked:** each application-form question you answered on the dashboard, with your
  answer, and the questions still waiting for one (with the jobs they held up).
- **Your history:** each job Sendoff looked at (title, company, link, the date, and what happened).
- **Your applications:** what you note about the jobs you applied to: when and how a company replied and who,
  the rounds and their dates, how it ended, your notes, and jobs you add yourself.
- **Referrals (LinkedIn):** the people you asked for a referral (name, headline, profile link, the job asked
  about, and whether they were messaged or invited), and the message and invite note you wrote.
- **Screenshots, only if you turn them on:** a picture of a job page that needed you.
- **Run progress:** what the current run is doing, cleared when Chrome closes.

## What Sendoff does with it

It uses this data only to do what you start it to do:

- **Searching and applying:** it reads the job sites' pages in a window it opens, and types your answers into
  their application forms, which sends them to that job site, just as if you had filled in the form yourself.
- **Referrals:** it sends the messages and invites you set up to the LinkedIn members it finds at the companies
  you applied to. By default it shows you each one, with who it goes to, and sends it only when you click Send.
- **The dashboard:** it shows your history, your applications and progress. A round you add to your calendar is
  downloaded as a file on your computer; nothing is sent to a calendar service.

Sendoff does not send your data to the developer or to anyone else. It does not sell it, use it for
advertising, or use it to decide creditworthiness or for any purpose unrelated to applying to jobs. It loads no
code from the internet: everything it runs is in the extension you installed.

## Permissions

- **hirist.tech, instahyre.com, wellfound.com, cutshort.io, linkedin.com, naukri.com:** to search for jobs, read job
  pages and fill in applications on those sites, and (on LinkedIn) send the referral messages you set up.
- **greenhouse.io, jobs.lever.co, jobs.ashbyhq.com, myworkdayjobs.com, myworkdaysite.com:** to fill in a company's own
  application form there, with your profile, resume and answers, and send it to that company. On Workday you sign in
  yourself; Sendoff never sees or keeps your password.
- **Tabs and scripting:** to open the window it works in, follow where its pages go, and read and fill those
  pages.
- **Storage:** to keep your settings and history on your computer.
- **Access to all sites, only if you allow screenshots:** Chrome requires it for taking a screenshot of a tab.
  Sendoff still only goes to the job sites above.

## Your control

- See and change everything on the dashboard: Settings for what you entered, History for what it did.
- **Download backup** saves your settings, history, applications and answers to a file on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
- Removing the extension from Chrome deletes everything it kept. You can also turn screenshots off at any time
  in Settings.
