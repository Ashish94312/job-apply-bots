# Sendoff privacy policy

*Effective 14 October 2026. Applies to the Sendoff Chrome extension.*

Sendoff applies to jobs for you on Hirist, Instahyre, Wellfound, Cutshort, LinkedIn, Naukri and Indeed, and on companies' own
application forms on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and Workday, and on LinkedIn can ask people for referrals and write to the recruiters hiring for the jobs. Your job settings and application history stay in Chrome. The edition with Account and billing also uses a merchant service to verify access.

## What Sendoff keeps, and where

The following job data is kept in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage`) on your computer:

- **Your settings:** the jobs to search for, your filters, and the answers you write for screening questions.
  These can include personal details you choose to enter, such as your phone number, city, college, notice
  period and salary.
- **Your Workday sign-in, only if you save one:** an email and password for companies' Workday sites, kept apart
  from your settings so it's never in a backup file, and never shown on the dashboard after you save it. It is stored
  unencrypted in this browser profile, so use a password just for these accounts. It's typed only into the Workday
  sign-in and create-account forms.
- **Your profile and resume:** the name, email, phone, city, country, links and current job you enter, and the resume
  file you add, for the application forms that ask for them.
- **Answers you gave when it asked:** each application-form question you answered on the dashboard, with your
  answer, and the questions still waiting for one (with the jobs they held up).
- **Your history:** each job Sendoff looked at (title, company, link, the date, and what happened).
- **Your applications:** what you note about the jobs you applied to: when and how a company replied and who,
  the rounds and their dates, how it ended, your notes, and jobs you add yourself.
- **Referrals and recruiters (LinkedIn):** the people you asked for a referral or wrote to as a recruiter (name,
  headline, profile link, the job it was about, and whether they were messaged or invited), and the messages and
  invite notes you wrote. Who counts as a recruiter is worked out in Chrome from their LinkedIn headline; nothing is
  sent anywhere to decide it.
- **Screenshots, only if you turn them on:** a picture of a job page that needed you.
- **Run progress:** what the current run is doing, cleared when Chrome closes.

## What Sendoff does with it

It uses this data only to do what you start it to do:

- **Searching and applying:** it reads the job sites' pages in a window it opens, and types your answers into
  their application forms, which sends them to that job site, just as if you had filled in the form yourself.
- **Referrals and recruiters:** it sends the messages and invites you set up to the LinkedIn members it finds at
  the companies you applied to: people there for a referral, or whoever posted the job or a recruiter there. By
  default it shows you each one, with who it goes to, and sends it only when you click Send.
- **The dashboard:** it shows your history, your applications and progress. A round you add to your calendar is
  downloaded as a file on your computer; nothing is sent to a calendar service.

Sendoff keeps job data apart from account and payment records. It does not sell it, use it for
advertising, or use it to decide creditworthiness or for any purpose unrelated to applying to jobs. It loads no
code from the internet: everything it runs is in the extension you installed.

## Account and billing

In the paid edition, all product features require an active pass. You sign in with your Google account to buy or restore one. Google tells the merchant service your account's identifier and email address, nothing else (no contacts, files or other Google data). The merchant service stores an identifier derived from your Google account, your email address, and for each purchase the product, price, payment and refund references, and access dates, so it can confirm purchases, restore them and answer support requests. The extension keeps a sign-in credential in Chrome's local extension storage (never in a backup file); it ends after 30 days without use. Signing out deletes it there and on the merchant service. Uninstalling removes local data. Purchase records are kept by the merchant so you can restore access and request support.

When you buy a pass, the merchant sends Cashfree the billing phone you enter, the customer identifier, order reference, amount and currency. You enter payment details on Cashfree's checkout. The billing service receives payment and refund status. Contact the seller shown at checkout for billing, privacy and deletion requests. Share order references with support privately.

## Permissions

- **Identity and the merchant HTTPS origin, in the billing edition:** to sign in with Google through your browser and check your pass. Cashfree runs on the merchant checkout website.

- **hirist.tech, instahyre.com, wellfound.com, cutshort.io, linkedin.com, naukri.com:** to search for jobs, read job
  pages and fill in applications on those sites, and (on LinkedIn) send the referral and recruiter messages you set up.
- **greenhouse.io, jobs.lever.co, jobs.ashbyhq.com, myworkdayjobs.com, myworkdaysite.com:** to fill in a company's own
  application form there, with your profile, resume and answers, and send it to that company. On Workday you sign in
  yourself, or with the sign-in you saved (above), which is typed only into Workday's own sign-in and create-account forms.
- **Tabs and scripting:** to open the window it works in, follow where its pages go, and read and fill those
  pages.
- **Storage:** to keep your settings and history on your computer.
- **indeed.com, only once you use Indeed:** to search Indeed for jobs, read job pages and fill in Indeed's own
  application, signed in with your Indeed account.
- **ycombinator.com and workatastartup.com, only once you use YC startups:** to read YC's public job pages and, signed
  in with your YC account, apply there with the note to the founders you saw (or chose to send straight away).
- **Access to all sites, only if you allow screenshots or other companies' forms:** Chrome requires it for taking a
  screenshot of a tab, and for filling in an application form on whatever site a company uses. It goes only to the
  job links you list and the ones the job sites led to.

## Your control

- See and change everything on the dashboard: Settings for what you entered, History for what it did.
- **Download backup** saves your settings, history, applications and answers (never your Workday sign-in) to a file on your computer; nothing is uploaded.
- Removing the extension deletes its local copy. You can also turn screenshots off at any time
  in Settings.
