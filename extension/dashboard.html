Job bots

Click Apply and a Chrome window opens on that site. The bot goes through jobs that match your settings and applies; when it needs you for a login, a check or a question, it asks here.

Before the first run, check Settings: the answers to screening questions (salary, notice period, phone…) are sample values. Coming from the Python bots? Bring your settings and history over.

Bring over from the Python bots

In the bots' folder, run .venv/bin/python tools/export_for_extension.py, then pick the extension-import.json it writes. Your settings are replaced by the ones in the file, and the jobs the Python bots already handled are added to the history, so they aren't applied to again.

Screenshots

Keep a screenshot of each job that needs you, to see what it asked. Chrome lets an extension take screenshots only if it may see every site, so it asks you for that; the bot still only goes to the job sites.

Back up

Download your settings and history as a file. Bring it back the same way as above, here or in another Chrome.
