                Your experience
                
              
                
                
                
                
              
                
              
                Hirist, Instahyre, Cutshort and Naukri only search jobs in your range (Naukri: jobs open to the top of it).
                
            
                

            
                
              
                Which jobs to apply to All sites
                
              
                Whole words, any case, one per line. A title must have one word from the first list and one from the second, and none from the third.
                
              
                
                
                
                
              
                
              
                
                
                
              
                
            
                

            
                
              
                Answers to screening questions All sites but LinkedIn, which has its own
                
              
                Each question is matched against these patterns from the top, and the first match answers it.
                A pattern is a few words or a regular expression, any case. For yes/no questions the answer has to be one of
                the options, like "Yes". Leave an answer empty to keep that question for yourself: the job is then marked
                Needs you. The bot never guesses.

                Screenshots
                
            
                Keep a screenshot of each job that needs you, to see what it asked. Chrome lets an extension take
              screenshots only if it may see every site, so turning this on asks you for that. The bot still only goes to the job sites.
                
            
                
              
                
          
                

          
                
            
                Bring over from the Python bots
                
            
                In the bots' folder, run .venv/bin/python tools/export_for_extension.py and pick the
              extension-import.json it writes. It replaces your settings here and adds the jobs the Python bots
              already handled to the history, so they aren't applied to again.
                
            
                
              
                
            
                Back up
                
            
                Download your settings and history as a file. Bring it back the same way, here or in another Chrome.
